# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers must communicate respectfully and professionally. Welcome
participants regardless of background, identity, experience, or disability. Critique
ideas and code constructively; avoid personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory
language, threats, and unwanted disclosure of private information.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Report
concerns privately to felipe.castillo@lynvera.ai. The maintainer will review reports,
protect confidentiality as far as practical, and respond proportionately. Responses may
include clarification, a warning, removal of content, or restricting participation.
Maintainers are subject to the same expectations.
